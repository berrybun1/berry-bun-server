🌟 The AFK Corner - Server Rules 🌟

Welcome to Berry Bun! We're part of The AFK Corner community. Please follow these essential rules to keep our grinding sessions smooth and fun for everyone!

01

NO TRADING AT ALL

Trading is strictly not allowed in grind servers. Save that for the main game!

02

NO AFK BABIES

No AFK babies allowed — actively playing is okay, but AFK babies will result in a kick!

03

ONE FAMILY ONLY

Everyone must be in ONE family — do not create multiple families or try to separate.

04

NO ORANGE TASKS

No orange tasks — short breaks for Pet Shop/Golden Apples are okay, but no tasks!

05

AFK = SEATED ONLY

If you're AFK, you MUST be sitting in a proper seat. Standing AFK is not allowed!

06

MOBILE USERS

Mobile users: change your name to "AFK" and make sure you follow all seating rules!

07

GRIND HOUSE LOCATION

The grind house is always on the main plot near the Hospital & Nursery. Head there first!

08

NO HIDING

No hiding behind trees, treadmills or anywhere you shouldn't be. Use proper seats only!

09

WAIT FOR SEATS

Join the family and wait for an open seat before grinding. Don't force your way in!

10

BE KIND & RESPECTFUL

Treat everyone with kindness and positivity. We're all here to have fun and grind together!

11

NO HARASSMENT

No harassment, bullying, or drama of any kind. Keep it friendly and chill!

12

RESPECT THE HOST

Respect the host and follow their instructions at all times. They keep things running!

13

CONSEQUENCES

Rule breaking may result in a warning, kick, or ban. Keep it chill! 🎀

📋 How to Join & Start Grinding

  1. Find an Open Server - Look for the green "Open" badge below
  2. Click Join Server - You'll be taken directly to the Roblox server
  3. Travel to the Grind House - It's on the main plot near Hospital & Nursery
  4. Accept the Family Invite - Wait for an active grinder to invite you (set family to "Everyone")
  5. Find an Available Seat - Make sure a seat is open before going AFK!

✨ Pro Tips:

  • ✓ Set your Adopt Me family permissions to "Everyone"
  • ✓ Follow host instructions for smoothest experience
  • ✓ Grab healing apples near the hospital!
  • ✓ Be patient — good vibes only! 🌸
